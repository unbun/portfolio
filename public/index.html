About

I'm a robotics engineer in Boston, working toward a master's in Robotics and Autonomous Systems at Johns Hopkins part-time and online. I studied Computer Engineering and Computer Science at Northeastern University (BSE 2021), and I've worked on robots and medical devices at Medtronic, Hologic, Amazon Robotics and GE.

I have always been interested in robotics and controls as the boundary where software meets the real world: kinematics, controls, perception, simulation, and the tools that make robots easier to build and test. With robotics, I am driven to make a positive impact on people's lives. Outside of work and school, I mentor a FIRST robotics team and have a number of small software projects for fun and to learn new things.
